---
name: Public facts vs. modeled economics
description: Durable evidence-governance rule for the Stargate Abilene diligence workbench.
---

Public reporting about Stargate Abilene should remain visibly separate from the workbench's synthetic acquisition economics. Preserve source qualifiers when reports conflict or do not substantiate a facility-level fact; water consumption and Taylor County water rights are intentionally unresolved until a public or diligence source establishes them.

**Why:** The workbench is meant to demonstrate how provenance changes investment conclusions, so presenting modeled values as public facts would undermine the core product behavior.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising Stargate inputs, use the five provenance classes and label representative capacity, cost, and return assumptions as synthetic rather than reported transaction terms.

Community agreement benchmarks follow the same boundary: a related or comparable public agreement can frame reviewer questions, but cannot become Stargate evidence, a fund-level risk rating, or an automatic return adjustment without attributable project support and human approval.

**Why:** Community provisions are often persuasive before they are project-specific; collapsing the layers would make a benchmark look like an executed Stargate commitment and create unsupported financial precision.

**How to apply:** Keep benchmark judgments, SafeLoc classifications, AI proposals, and human conclusions visibly separate; route unresolved terms through diligence gaps and do not invent IRR penalties for missing stewardship documentation.
