---
name: Acquisition ranking boundary
description: Why discovery specificity only determines open preference, and how opaque metadata limits diagnosis.
---

Pre-open project specificity is an acquisition preference, never verified identity or evidence. Do not compensate for opaque redirect metadata by inventing destination URLs or snippets.

**Why:** A bounded Red Oak retrieval exhausted its opens before producing an identity/Grid packet. The retained report lacked several receipts and original ordering; neither improved ranking nor later document text can establish the missing historical pre-open facts.

**How to apply:** Preserve discovery order separately from selection priority and admission decisions, use only available submitted-project and candidate metadata, and leave unavailable historical telemetry explicitly unavailable. Retrieval-only diagnostics stop before structured analysis even if exact-project text is obtained.

Audit capacity must follow the discovery bound, not the smaller acquisition or physical-open limit. Verify the finalized repository audit, not only response coverage or a diagnostic collector.

**Why:** A full bounded discovery batch can contain many deferred candidates. Duplicate deferred entries and admission-sized audit truncation hid both later candidates and actual open receipts despite a complete response-level list.

**How to apply:** Retain one decision/outcome per discovered candidate and every governed open receipt; exercise a maximum discovery batch with smaller admission/open limits in offline finalization regressions.
