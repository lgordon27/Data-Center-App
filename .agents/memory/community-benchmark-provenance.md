---
name: Community benchmark provenance
description: Durable rules for external community-agreement benchmarks and project/entity resolution.
---

External community-agreement records are benchmark context, not project evidence. Keep imported statuses, source identity, links, quotations, summaries, and verification metadata immutable and separate from analyst conclusions and classifications.

**Why:** A benchmark can name related campus entities without proving that every phase, operator, or adjacent capacity shares one agreement. Missing primary documents and exact quotations must remain visible gaps rather than being filled with generated text.

**How to apply:** Use source-record anchors only when verified, render “No exact quotation captured” when no verbatim text is stored, show primary-document links only when validated, and represent entity relationships with explicit type, confidence, reasoning, supporting sources, and an unverified state.

Community documentation types require affirmative support from the retained source passage, not just generated narration or a source URL. Absence of an executed agreement does not prove that a proposed agreement exists.

**Why:** Community summaries can accidentally turn a missing signature into a proposal, or a reviewed benchmark into a binding project obligation. Both overstate what the documents establish.

**How to apply:** Keep executed, proposed, voluntary, regulatory, public-commitment and opposition evidence distinct. Test negation and narration/source contradictions before changing summaries.
