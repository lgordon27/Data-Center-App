# Part 1: Rewrite How It Works

## Length and hierarchy

The collapsed/default How It Works experience should fit within approximately two to three desktop viewports.

The user should understand the product before opening any disclosure.

Use progressive disclosure for methodology and technical material.

## Hero

Use this content:

### Headline

> See how an infrastructure constraint could become an investment question.

### Description

> Start with a public company, inspect the project evidence, trace the possible financial pathway, and leave with questions an advisor or investor can use.

### Primary CTA

> Open the Stargate demonstration

The CTA must open the curated Stargate analysis with Oracle selected, matching the homepage Stargate demonstration behavior.

## Four-stage tour

Show four compact cards in this order.

### 1. Market Exposure

> Establish the documented relationship between a public company and the infrastructure project.

CTA:

> Open Market Exposure

### 2. Project Reality

> Separate verified project facts from unresolved power, water, construction, climate and community claims.

CTA:

> Open Project Reality

### 3. Financial Transmission

> Trace how a physical constraint could affect project timing, costs, an issuer and ultimately a portfolio.

CTA:

> Open Financial Transmission

### 4. Advisor Brief

> Convert the analysis into concise questions for a company, fund manager or client conversation.

CTA:

> Open Advisor Brief

Each card may include one optional `Learn more` disclosure. Do not display long explanatory paragraphs by default.

## CTA behavior

Each CTA must:

* Open `#analysis`.
* Activate the correct current tab.
* Move keyboard focus to the selected tab or its primary heading.
* Respect reduced-motion preferences.
* Avoid restoring an obsolete route or anchor.
* Preserve the curated Stargate case.
* Preserve Oracle as the curated starting company.

Do not create a second source of truth for tab state. Use the existing analysis navigation/state mechanism.

## Progressive-disclosure sections

Move these topics below the four-stage tour and collapse them by default:

* Evidence-classification methodology
* Data-source methodology
* Provider freshness
* Financial-model methodology
* Technical architecture
* AI research process
* Builder background
* Detailed market timeline and statistics

Each disclosure should have a clear title and one-sentence preview.

Do not remove source access or substantive methodology. Reduce its default visual prominence.

## Remove outdated product framing

Remove prominent language such as:

* Governed financial model
* AI proposes. You decide.
* The financial model only moves when the human acts.
* Human approval at the point where judgment enters
* Approval history
* Authorization workflow
* CAM-related product explanation

Replace it with one concise statement:

> AI helps locate and organize public evidence. Sources, assumptions and unresolved gaps remain visible and correctable.

Do not remove evidence provenance, source distinctions or the separation between verified, inferred, assumed and missing information.

## Reduce repetition

Do not repeat the same:

* $130 billion statistic
* ERCOT queue statistic
* Texas moratorium statement
* Sustainable-investor narrative
* Synthetic-economics warning

If a statistic is retained, show it once with its source and date.

# Part 2: Simplify AI Chain

## Above the fold

The chain visualization should appear before long narrative or supporting statistics.

Use:

### Headline

> Follow AI demand from chips to client portfolios.

### Description

> SafeLoc focuses on the infrastructure layer, where power, water, grid, construction and community constraints can interrupt the chain.

Then display the seven-stage chain immediately.

## Seven-stage presentation

Preserve the existing seven stages and their substantive meaning.

In the default view, each stage should show only:

* Stage number
* Stage name
* One-line description
* Expand control

Expand Data Center Infrastructure by default.

Collapse the following for all other stages:

* Player lists
* Long explanations
* Evidence lists
* Market statistics
* Source collections

Opening one stage should not automatically expand every stage. Prefer one expanded stage at a time.

## Infrastructure stage

The expanded Data Center Infrastructure stage should explain, concisely:

* This is where AI demand encounters power, water, grid, construction and community constraints.
* SafeLoc begins its analysis at this layer.
* Project-level evidence does not automatically establish issuer or portfolio materiality.

Keep relevant source links accessible inside the expanded content.

## Responsible-investing connection

Mention the values-aligned-investing connection once:

> Investors evaluating AI exposure increasingly need to understand the physical infrastructure and community conditions beneath public-company growth assumptions.

Do not repeatedly state that sustainable investors financed or capitalized the AI revolution.

## Investment boundary

Include one concise qualifier:

> A documented infrastructure relationship is a starting point for diligence, not proof of security-level or fund-level materiality.
