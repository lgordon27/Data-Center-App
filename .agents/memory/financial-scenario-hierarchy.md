---
name: Financial scenario hierarchy
description: Stable hierarchy between synthetic diligence economics and provider electricity sensitivities.
---

Use the synthetic current-evidence scenario as the primary diligence and recommendation basis. Keep its fully verified synthetic counterpart as the underwriting benchmark. Provider-backed electricity scenarios are separate sensitivities and must not silently replace the primary case.

**Why:** Provider availability is transient and the statewide EIA industrial observation is not a disclosed Stargate tariff or contracted project price. Letting provider state choose the primary return made the recommendation unstable and overstated project specificity.

**How to apply:** Compatibility scenarios, captures and saved scenarios should name their evidence and electricity basis explicitly. Preserve raw and applied provider inputs, and keep unavailable or refreshing provider states from changing synthetic metrics or recommendation status.

The owned estimated-return view is an exception to the synthetic-primary presentation rule: use traceable reported financing/capacity and labeled operating benchmarks. Treat utilities as assumed tenant pass-through and admit only genuine state-matched EIA receipts for tenant context. Do not silently rewrite unrelated recommendation or review surfaces.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested estimated financial ranges while restricting ownership and preserving evidence-acceptance contracts. Reported inputs do not make the resulting modeled return a disclosed project term.

**How to apply:** Keep personal assumption edits and their audit in the browser session, separate from canonical evidence acceptance. Report inherited exact-return surfaces outside ownership instead of modifying them without permission.
