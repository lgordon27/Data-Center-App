## Objective

Add a Texas-first Community Agreement Review capability to SafeLoc Diligence Workbench.

The application should:

1. Prioritize Texas data-center projects.
2. Use the 15 agreements published by Future Pickleball Court as a national benchmark corpus.
3. Connect agreements to projects only when evidence supports the relationship.
4. Provide national comparisons when no direct Texas agreement is available.
5. Treat missing public documentation as a diligence gap, not proof that no agreement exists.
6. Separate financial drivers, decision gates, stewardship indicators, and contextual information.
7. Require human acceptance before an AI assessment changes the project analysis.

This is an evidence-intelligence enhancement, not a new standalone community database.

## Project-to-Agreement Relationships

Use four explicit relationship classifications:

### Direct

The agreement or primary document explicitly applies to the analyzed project.

### Related

The document involves the same campus, operator, developer, utility, or jurisdiction, but direct applicability is not conclusively established.

Display: `Related record, review required`

### Comparable

The agreement belongs to another project and is used only as a benchmark.

Display: `National benchmark, not a term of this project`

### Not found

No applicable agreement was located in the available corpus.

Display: `No direct public agreement located in the available sources`

Never display:

* “No agreement exists”
* “The project has no community protections”
* “The company failed to negotiate an agreement”

unless supported by verified primary evidence.

## Financial Treatment

Every community term must be assigned one of four treatments.

### Financial driver

May change a financial assumption when explicitly quantified.

Potential examples:

* Tax incentives
* Grid-cost allocation
* Water infrastructure
* Road-repair obligations
* Quantifiable clawbacks
* Decommissioning security
* Evidence-supported permitting delay

### Decision gate

May block or condition readiness without automatically changing IRR.

### Stewardship indicator

Relevant to responsible-investment engagement but does not automatically change project returns.

### Context

Informational evidence that does not affect returns or recommendation by itself.

Community funds, resident representation, transparency, and similar factors must not receive invented IRR penalties.
