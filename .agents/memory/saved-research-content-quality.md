---
name: Saved research content quality
description: Recheck legacy captured passages at use time without rewriting their immutable fetch receipts.
---

A saved `accessible`/`extracted` receipt is a historical transport observation,
not a permanent guarantee that its text is usable. Apply current bounded
content-quality checks before displaying retained findings, selecting structured
analysis input, or granting proposal eligibility, while preserving original
receipts and recording exclusion reasons separately.

**Why:** Application-error chrome and corrupted PDF text can survive an older
extractor and later bypass newly corrected extraction simply because they were
already stored. Discarding the receipt would hide the origin of that content.

**How to apply:** Share passage-quality checks across server selection,
saved-result containment, client response normalization, and browser-session
restoration. Response parsing does not cover directly restored project state.
Never rewrite historical
records to retrofit new access outcomes; keep quality exclusion distinct from
transport inaccessibility.
