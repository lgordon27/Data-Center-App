---
name: Financial scenario hierarchy
description: Stable hierarchy between synthetic diligence economics and provider electricity sensitivities.
---

Use the synthetic current-evidence scenario as the primary diligence and recommendation basis. Keep its fully verified synthetic counterpart as the underwriting benchmark. Provider-backed electricity scenarios are separate sensitivities and must not silently replace the primary case.

**Why:** Provider availability is transient and the statewide EIA industrial observation is not a disclosed Stargate tariff or contracted project price. Letting provider state choose the primary return made the recommendation unstable and overstated project specificity.

**How to apply:** New financial views, exports, captures, and saved scenarios should name their evidence and electricity basis explicitly. Preserve raw and applied provider inputs, and keep unavailable or refreshing provider states from changing synthetic metrics or recommendation status.
