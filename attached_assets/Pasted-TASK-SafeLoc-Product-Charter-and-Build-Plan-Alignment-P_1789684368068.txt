1. PRIMARY PRODUCT GOAL

SafeLoc helps financial advisors and asset managers trace a public investment holding to the physical AI infrastructure underneath it, evaluate the evidence behind that infrastructure and understand what the project-level conditions could mean for investment conversations, monitoring and engagement.

The intended chain is:

Holding or fund
→ issuer
→ issuer-project relationship
→ physical project evidence
→ project financial sensitivity
→ issuer and market transmission
→ Financial Advisor or Asset Manager output

The project analysis is the evidence engine. The FA and Asset Manager outputs are the product.

The app must not become primarily:

* A data-center directory
* A generic web-research agent
* A project-finance underwriting platform
* A community-agreement scorecard
* An ESG ratings dashboard
* A document summarizer
* A CAM or authorization demonstration

2. PRIMARY USERS

Financial Advisors

Financial Advisors need to understand:

* What their clients own
* How a holding connects to AI infrastructure
* What physical or community constraints exist
* Which conclusions are verified
* What remains unknown
* Whether issuer-level financial materiality has been established
* How to discuss the issue with a client
* What should be monitored or raised through fund managers

The FA output should be concise, plain-language and client-ready.

It should not provide automatic buy or sell recommendations.

Asset Managers

Asset Managers need:

* Issuer-project relationship mapping
* Exact project evidence and provenance
* Project-level constraints
* Cross-project patterns
* Project financial sensitivities
* Issuer-materiality assessment
* Evidence gaps
* Engagement questions
* Disclosure requests
* Monitoring triggers
* A provenance appendix

The Asset Manager output should be more analytical and evidence-dense than the FA output.

Secondary audience

Project investors, consultants and infrastructure professionals may find the project analysis useful, but they are not the primary conference or initial product audience.

3. EXISTING FOUR-STAGE PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Keep the existing four-stage workbench. Do not add new top-level workbench stages.

Stage 1: Market Exposure

This stage starts with what the investor owns.

It should answer:

* Which holding or fund is being examined?
* Which infrastructure projects are connected to it?
* What is the relationship type?
* Is the relationship direct, indirect or thematic?
* What evidence supports the relationship?
* How confident is SafeLoc in the relationship?
* Is the project potentially material to the issuer, or is materiality still unknown?

Directory operator matching must not automatically become a contractual or causal relationship.

Stage 2: Project Reality

This stage shows the material physical and community conditions:

* Power and grid interconnection
* Electricity cost
* Water and cooling
* Construction and schedule
* Permitting
* Climate and operational resilience
* Counterparties
* Community agreements
* Missing evidence

Detailed source provenance should remain available through progressive disclosure.

The default experience should be concise and visual.

Stage 3: Financial Transmission

This stage must maintain a clear distinction between:

Project economics

* Delay
* CAPEX escalation
* Power-price sensitivity
* Downtime
* Revenue deferral
* Project IRR and value sensitivity

Market transmission

* Which issuer is exposed
* Nature of the exposure
* Possible earnings, CAPEX, demand, reliability or reputation channel
* Whether the effect is quantified, directional or unknown
* What additional evidence is needed to establish issuer materiality

Project IRR must never be presented as issuer return, fund return or client portfolio return.

Stage 4: Advisor Brief

This stage must provide two substantively different outputs:

* Financial Advisor
* Asset Manager

Changing the audience must change the content, questions, metrics, evidence depth and next actions. It cannot merely change a label.

6. CORRECT ROLE OF THE RESEARCH AGENT

The research agent should not be the sole source of every project dossier.

Its intended role is:

* Load existing project evidence
* Check freshness
* Search missing or stale categories
* Retrieve new official documents
* Propose new or changed claims
* Explain conflicts and limitations
* Preserve exact source passages
* Wait for reviewer acceptance
* Update the persistent dossier after acceptance

The agent is a bounded research, refresh and gap-filling agent.

It is not an autonomous investment recommender.

It must not automatically change financial inputs.

7. FACILITY COVERAGE MODEL

The directory and the evidence registry are different products underneath the same interface.

The current Compute Atlas integration reportedly contains approximately 1,500 facilities. Verify the actual current count and source.

Those records establish a project universe. They do not mean that every facility has a complete evidence dossier.

Every project must have one explicit coverage state:

Evidence reviewed

A sufficiently sourced and reviewed dossier supports the complete four-stage workflow.

Evidence available

Some source-backed findings exist, but important categories remain unresolved.

Discovery record

Facility identity and directory context exist, but an evidence-reviewed dossier has not been established.

Research underway

A bounded research job is actively refreshing or building evidence.

Research incomplete

The run ended without enough eligible evidence. Existing accepted evidence remains available, and unsupported conclusions remain absent.

A directory-only facility must never appear equally researched as a reviewed case.

11. EVIDENCE AND MODEL BOUNDARY

The intended lifecycle is:

Discovered
→ retrieved
→ extracted
→ proposed
→ eligible
→ accepted, rejected or unresolved
→ superseded when newer evidence is accepted

Only accepted, model-compatible claims may affect project financial inputs.

A claim requires:

* Exact facility identity
* Eligible source authority
* Exact retained passage or equivalent structured record
* Applicable date and scope
* Compatible value and unit where relevant
* Existing evidence-variable mapping
* Explicit reviewer acceptance

Directory metadata, source discovery, company-name matching and model-generated inference are not accepted evidence by themselves.

13. PROJECT-TO-MARKET TRANSMISSION

SafeLoc must explicitly separate:

Relationship confidence

Is the company actually connected to this project?

Project materiality

Is the physical constraint significant to the project?

Issuer materiality

Is this project or cross-project pattern large enough to matter to the public company?

Portfolio materiality

Is the issuer exposure meaningful to the fund or client portfolio?

Permitted materiality states should include:

* Quantified
* Directional
* Exposure established but financial materiality unquantified
* Relationship unverified
* No supported transmission established

* CAPEX escalation
* Revenue deferral
* Asset utilization
* AI capacity availability
* Supplier demand
* Operating margin
* Reliability
* Regulatory exposure
* Community opposition
* Reputation
* Stranded capital
