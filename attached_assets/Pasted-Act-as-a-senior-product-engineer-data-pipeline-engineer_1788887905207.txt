Act as a senior product engineer, data-pipeline engineer and skeptical investment-product reviewer. Audit the entire SafeLoc Diligence Workbench repository and deployed experience before changing any code.

Do not implement fixes during this first pass.

CONTEXT

The application is intended to:

* Trace public companies to AI infrastructure projects
* Research project-specific public evidence
* Classify evidence into five provenance categories
* Connect evidence quality to financial assumptions
* Require human approval before AI proposals affect accepted analysis
* Serve financial advisors, asset managers and infrastructure investors
* Prioritize Texas data centers before nationwide expansion

The current product has three major failures:

1. Custom project research retrieves too few validated sources.
2. The application presents far too much text and supporting detail at once.
3. The homepage redesign did not materially simplify or demonstrate the product.

AUDIT REQUIREMENTS

1. Run the application and all existing tests.
2. Inspect the live deployed experience and local application at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
3. Trace the complete curated Stargate case.
4. Trace at least two custom Texas project searches.
5. Record all provider requests, responses, errors, timeouts and fallback behavior.
6. Inspect how source results become evidence records.
7. Inspect how evidence records become financial assumptions.
8. Inspect how agent proposals become, or fail to become, accepted state.
9. Inspect every route and homepage CTA.
10. Measure the rendered height of Home and every Analysis section.
11. Identify duplicate content, duplicate CTAs and permanently expanded supporting material.
12. Compare the current homepage against its stated acceptance criteria.
13. Identify stale, hard-coded or contradictory metrics.
14. Identify tests that pass despite broken user-facing behavior.

SOURCE-PIPELINE QUESTIONS

Determine and report:

* What search or research provider is actually called?
* Does it perform live retrieval, or rely on model knowledge?
* How many queries are issued per project?
* What are the generated queries?
* Are queries tailored by evidence category?
* How many results are returned?
* How many results are discarded?
* Why is each result discarded?
* Are redirects followed?
* Are PDFs and government documents processed?
* Are JavaScript-rendered pages supported?
* Are domains restricted?
* Are request failures surfaced or silently swallowed?
* Are timeouts, rate limits or API failures visible?
* Are duplicate sources removed correctly?
* Can one source support multiple evidence variables?
* Does entity resolution reject sources using alternate project, campus or developer names?
* Can the system distinguish a facility, campus, project phase, developer, operator, tenant and supplier?
* Does zero-source research fail closed?
* Can unsupported generated prose enter the project summary?
* Can unsupported content become Verified Evidence?

For each custom project tested, provide:

* Queries sent
* Providers called
* Raw result count
* Parsed result count
* Validated source count
* Rejected source count
* Rejection reasons
* Evidence-variable coverage
* Final confidence
* Any generated statements lacking support

UX QUESTIONS

Determine and report:

* How many viewport lengths does each page and section require?
* Which content is permanently expanded?
* Which purported tabs are not functional?
* Which controls have unclear outcomes?
* Which content repeats elsewhere?
* Which sections should become tabs, tables, accordions or drawers?
* Does each section have one obvious next action?
* Do cross-page deep links reach the requested section?
* Do sticky headers cover anchor targets?
* Is mobile navigation usable?
* Is there horizontal overflow?
* Does the product feel like a tool or a long research report?

HOMEPAGE QUESTIONS

Determine and report:

* What can a visitor understand within ten seconds?
* Is the actual product demonstrated above the fold?
* How many competing entry paths are presented?
* Why did the previous redesign leave most of the original structure intact?
* Is the hero’s right side being used?
* Does Start with NVIDIA behave as its label suggests?
* Are holdings-first and project-first journeys clearly distinguished?
* Are custom-project forms or Stargate CTAs duplicated?
* Are legacy header elements still present?
* Are dynamically changing figures hard-coded?
* Which requested acceptance criteria were not implemented?
