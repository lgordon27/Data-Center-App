---
name: Recurring attribution semantics
description: How to label annual financial-driver effects without conflating operating impact and exit proceeds.
---

An annual driver effect should come from the affected recurring operating line, not the final-period equity cash flow. Full dollar impact and IRR sensitivity may include terminal value and debt repayment, but recurring annual figures must exclude them.

**Why:** Year 5 equity cash flow includes exit proceeds, so using its delta as an annual OPEX effect materially overstates recurring impact.

**How to apply:** Derive recurring annual values from the mapped revenue or OPEX field, label close-only inputs as having no recurring annual effect, and keep total equity cash-flow attribution separately labeled.
